Padel Americano schedule for 8 players (printable)

By the developer of Smash! Padel Manager · Updated 17 October 2026

The short answer: 8 players, 2 courts, 7 rounds. In this schedule every player partners every other player exactly once and plays against every other player exactly twice, and nobody sits out. Type your players’ names in the boxes to fill the table in, then print it.

How to run it

  1. Give each player a letter (draw them from a hat, or just go in order of arrival).
  2. Play round 1: A and B against C and D on Court 1, E and F against G and H on Court 2.
  3. Play every match to the same number of points (see the scoring rules), 24 is a good default, and write the score in the box.
  4. Each player adds up their own points across the 7 rounds. Most points wins; point difference breaks ties.

At about 12 minutes a round the whole evening takes a little under an hour and a half.

Why this schedule is fair

  • Over the 7 rounds you partner each of the other 7 players exactly once.
  • You face each of the other 7 players exactly twice.
  • Everybody plays all 7 rounds, so everybody plays the same number of matches.

We checked this by computer, rather than by eye, before publishing: every pair of players partners once and opposes twice.
